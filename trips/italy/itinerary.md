WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION
 - ONE DAY ON, ONE DAY OFF. After the two arrival days the trip alternates: a driving day, then a
   day that stays at the house (pool, terrace, a walk — no car). Off days: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13.
 - Every driving day now routes past a real FARMERS MARKET or producer shop, with opening hours
   checked for that exact date (26 Sep 2026). They're the 🧺 "Markets & produce" layer on the map;
   each name in the day text has a 📍 link straight into Google Maps.
 - Argentario: Feniglia + Tarocchi + Orbetello's lagoon co-op merged into one southern day (Day 4).
   Parco della Maremma is now the swap option, not a fixed day.
 - Chianti: Greve's Saturday market + Panzano + Volpaia on Day 8; Siena + Monteriggioni on Day 10.
   San Gimignano is dropped — too far to pair with Siena without wrecking the day.

   (Day 14). Montepulciano dropped; Bagno Vignoni becomes an easy evening soak on the off day.
 - Pin fixes from a map check: Giardino dei Tarocchi (was ~4 km off), Bagni San Filippo (~7 km off),
   Sant'Antimo, Monte Oliveto, Vitaleta, Feniglia.

DAY 2 · Sun 4 Oct — ON · Pick up the car, the coast road south, stock the house  ›› arrive & check in

NOTES
 - RHYTHM: on-days 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 15 · off-days 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13. Off means the car
   stays parked; anything listed on an off day is a walk or an optional ≤15-min hop.
 - MARKETS & SHOPS: hours were checked on 26 Sep 2026 against official sites, comune market
   calendars and Google listings. Where a note says "call" or "not confirmed", the sources disagreed.
   Italian shops shut ~13:00–16:00 — plan produce stops for mornings or after 16:00.
 - THE CAR — book it for Sun 4 Oct → Sat 17 Oct, same location both ends (round-trip, no
   one-way fee). Details:
     · PICK UP Sun 4 Oct, 09:00 at FIRENZE AIRPORT (FLR): the city-centre desks keep short or closed
       Sunday hours, FLR opens daily from ~08:00 and sits outside the ZTL. Tram T2 from SMN, ~20 min.
     · DROP Sat 17 Oct ~10:30. Rental days are 24 h blocks: 04 Oct 09:00 → 17 Oct 09:00 is exactly
       13 days, so a 10:30 drop is a 14th day (~€40–70). Book the 14 days.
     · Book a COMPACT — the Chianti back-roads and the lane up to Montelodoli are narrow, and
       every hill town wants you parked outside the walls. Automatics are rare: book one now if needed.
     · ZTL everywhere: park OUTSIDE the walls at Siena, Greve, Montalcino, Pienza, Buonconvento,
       Radda. Camera fines arrive by post months later.
 - Lodging: all three countryside stays are BOOKED. Still open — the Florence arrival night
   (3–4 Oct) only.
 - The Argentario house is SELF-CATERING and up on the hill at Pozzarello, ~2.5 km above Porto
   Santo Stefano harbour. Sunday-afternoon shopping in town is thin — stock up on the way in (Day 2).
 - Montelodoli Capanna: stone house for two, 16 m infinity pool 40 m from the door (season runs

   Brunello/Vino Nobile tastings they arrange.
 - The LAST NIGHT is a transit night: evening Frecciarossa to Milano Centrale (~2 h), then a late
   Malpensa Express or night bus to MXP. Terminals stay open — be there by ~05:00 for 07:00.
 - Trains: Day 1 (Milan → Florence) and Day 15 evening (Florence → Milano Centrale → Malpensa).
   Book now — "Super Economy" fares go fast.
 - Seasonal: the Giardino dei Tarocchi closes for the season 15 Oct (Day 4 is well inside).
   Montalcino's weekly market is FRIDAY (Day 14); Greve's is SATURDAY (Day 8); Grosseto's farmers
   market is THURSDAY (Day 6). The Sagra del Tordo (23–25 Oct), the San Giovanni d'Asso truffle fair
   (Nov) and Florence's Fierucola (Sun 18 Oct) all fall just outside the trip.
 - Baggage: the dawn flight is strict — ship a case of wine home (Day 14, Enoteca Bruno Dalmazio)
   or pre-book a checked bag. Cheese: ask for it vacuum-packed ("sottovuoto").
