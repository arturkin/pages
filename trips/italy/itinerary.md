DAY 2 · Sun 4 Oct — Pick up the car, the long coast road south  ›› arrive & check in
 - Collect the rental ~09:00 (Florence airport FLR is the safe Sunday desk — tram T2 from SMN,
   ~20 min — and it keeps you out of the ZTL entirely)
 - Confirm the drop is back at the SAME location on the contract (round-trip, no one-way fee)
 - Take the scenic way down the SS1 Aurelia: Bolgheri — the cypress avenue (Viale dei Cipressi)
   and a Super Tuscan stop (Sassicaia / Ornellaia country; you're driving — taste light or just buy)
 - Late lunch at Castiglione della Pescaia (medieval seaside town)
 - Groceries in Porto Santo Stefano before you climb to the house — it's self-catering
 - Check in at Poggio Calvello; first evening on the terrace, seafood down in the harbour
 >> Florence → Bolgheri → Castiglione della Pescaia → Porto Santo Stefano · ~4 h 15 driving + stops
    (the trip's longest day — if the pickup runs late, skip Bolgheri and go direct, ~2 h 50)

DAY 3 · Mon 5 Oct — Feniglia beach + the Tarocchi
 - Spiaggia della Feniglia (~25 min): long pine-backed beach; walk or bike the Duna Feniglia reserve
 - Giardino dei Tarocchi at Capalbio (Niki de Saint Phalle's mosaic park) — afternoons only;
   the season runs to 15 Oct, so early October is comfortably inside it
 - Aperitivo back on the Argentario — Bar Il Buco over the harbour

DAY 4 · Tue 6 Oct — Parco della Maremma
 - Parco della Maremma (Alberese, ~45 min): coastal trails to the towers and the wild beach,
   Maremmana cattle
 - Lagoon seafood and Orbetello bottarga for dinner (L'Oste Dispensa on the Giannella spit)

DAY 5 · Wed 7 Oct — Boat, coves, or the lagoon
 - Boat around the Argentario coves, or the footpath down to Cala del Gesso
 - OR Orbetello lagoon WWF reserve (flamingos) + the Spanish Fortress viewpoint right above you
   in Porto Santo Stefano
 - OR go inland (~1 h) to Massa Marittima: an underrated medieval town in the Colline Metallifere,
   dramatic cathedral, few tourists
 - Last Maremma swim; eat down the fridge; pack (into the hills tomorrow)

DAY 6 · Thu 8 Oct — Argentario → Chianti, through the wild interior  ›› arrive & check in
 - Take the inland route up rather than the motorway — the direct run is only ~2 h 10, so
   there's room for two good stops
 - Bagni di Petriolo (~1 h 20): a soak in the wild free sulphur springs in the river gorge under
   the old bridge (no facilities — bring water shoes; skip it if you'd rather push on)
 - Abbazia di San Galgano: the roofless Gothic abbey open to the sky, + the sword in the stone up
   at the Montesiepi chapel (abbey ~€6, open ~09:00–19:00; the chapel is free)
 - Groceries: the COOP in Gaiole in Chianti (11 km) — or a bigger shop in Siena as you pass
 - Check in at Montelodoli Capanna; the infinity pool, vines to the horizon, first quiet night
 >> Porto Santo Stefano → Petriolo → San Galgano → Monti in Chianti · ~3 h driving + stops

DAY 7 · Fri 9 Oct — Castello di Brolio, on your doorstep
 - Slow first morning at the house: the 16 m infinity pool, the Chianti hills
 - Castello di Brolio (2 km — a 10-min drive, or walk it): the Ricasoli castle where the Chianti
   recipe was written. Gardens, cellar tour + tasting, and the Osteria del Castello for lunch
 - The two hamlets at your gate: San Regolo and Monti in Chianti
 - Mid-October is the tail of the VENDEMMIA — cellars are working and the whole valley smells of
   must. Book every tasting ahead; a few estates close to visitors during picking

DAY 8 · Sat 10 Oct — Gaiole, Coltibuono & Radda
 - Gaiole in Chianti (12 min): Saturday morning in the little market town, the COOP, cafés
 - Castello di Meleto (10 min): another Ricasoli-era castle, cellar + frescoed rooms
 - Badia a Coltibuono (17 min): 11th-c. abbey turned wine estate — monastic garden, cellar
   tasting, one of Chianti's best-known restaurants (book)
 - Radda in Chianti (20 min): the ring-road walk around the walls, Casa Chianti Classico
 - Home for sunset and the pool

DAY 9 · Sun 11 Oct — Siena
 - Into Siena (~28 min): Piazza del Campo, the Duomo, the contrade lanes — a city, but a
   glorious walkable one. Park OUTSIDE the walls (Santa Caterina / Il Campo garages)
 - Lunch at Enoteca I Terzi, just off the Campo
 - Early evening back to the quiet; aperitivo at the house
 - (Skip it entirely if you'd rather just do nothing in the hills — no obligation)

DAY 10 · Mon 12 Oct — San Gimignano & Monteriggioni
 - Out west (~1 h 10) to San Gimignano (park outside the walls): the towers, a Vernaccia, lunch
 - Monteriggioni on the way back (~40 min from home) — the circular walled castle-village,
   aperitivo in the garden at Antico Travaglio
 - Quiet night in

DAY 11 · Tue 13 Oct — The Chiantigiana north, or nothing at all
 - Option A — the SR222 loop: Castello di Volpaia (27 min, a whole wine village), Panzano for
   Dario Cecchini's Antica Macelleria, Greve in Chianti's arcaded square (44 min)
 - Option B — do nothing. The pool, a book, an aperitivo on the terrace. This is what six
   nights in one house is for
 - Last Chianti dinner; pack for the move south

DAY 12 · Wed 14 Oct — Chianti → Val d'Orcia, across the Crete Senesi  ›› arrive & check in
 - Drop south past Siena into the clay hills — the biancane badlands, big empty skies
 - Asciano (~45 min), then Monte Oliveto Maggiore (15 min more): the great abbey in its
   cypress wood, Signorelli & Sodoma frescoes
 - Buonconvento: little walled town, good market — produce, pecorino, new-harvest oil
 - On to Locanda in Tuscany at Gallina; check in, the 20 m pool, sunset over the Val d'Orcia
 - Dinner at the Taverna di Mozart, the hotel's own restaurant in the old stable
 >> Monti in Chianti → Asciano → Monte Oliveto → Buonconvento → Castiglione d'Orcia · ~2 h driving + stops

DAY 13 · Thu 15 Oct — Pienza & Montepulciano (Vino Nobile)
 - Pienza (~30 min): cheese shops, the "ideal city" streets, Piccolomini palace, and the
   Romanesque Pieve di Corsignano just below the walls
 - Montepulciano (~40 min): Piazza Grande; Vino Nobile cellars carved under the town-centre palazzi
 - Cypress viewpoints on the way back at late light: Cappella di Vitaleta, Podere Belvedere,
   the "Gladiator" road
 - Dinner at Osteria La Porta in Monticchiello (book the sunset terrace), or back at the Locanda

DAY 14 · Fri 16 Oct — Montalcino market day, Sant'Antimo & the hot springs
 - Montalcino (~40 min): the FRIDAY market (produce, cheese, new-harvest oil), the Fortezza,
   a Brunello flight at Enoteca Osticcio's panoramic terrace
 - The Romanesque Abbey of Sant'Antimo just below the town
 - On the way back: Bagni San Filippo's white travertine "Fosso Bianco" (~25 min), or the free
   Parco dei Mulini pools under Bagno Vignoni (~17 min)
 - Rocca d'Orcia + Castiglione d'Orcia (10 min from the hotel) at last light
 - Buy/ship wine + oil TODAY (you fly at dawn on Sunday); last countryside dinner; pack

NOTES
 - THE CAR — book it for Sun 4 Oct → Sat 17 Oct, same location both ends (round-trip, no
   one-way fee). Details:
     · PICK UP Sun 4 Oct, 09:00. Prefer FIRENZE AIRPORT (FLR), not the SMN downtown desks:
       the city-centre offices on Borgo Ognissanti keep short or closed Sunday hours, while the
       airport desks open daily from ~08:00. FLR is also outside the ZTL, so you never have to
       thread the restricted zone with a fresh rental. Tram T2 from SMN to the airport, ~20 min.
     · DROP Sat 17 Oct. Rental days are 24 h blocks: 04 Oct 09:00 → 17 Oct 09:00 is exactly
       13 days; anything past ~09:30 on the 17th tips you into a 14th day. Leaving the Locanda
       after breakfast puts you in Florence ~11:00, so either book 13 days and leave at 07:00,
       or just book the 14th day (~€40–70) and drop at 11:00–12:00. Book the 14 days.
     · Book a COMPACT — the Chianti back-roads and the lane up to Montelodoli are narrow, and
       every hill town wants you parked outside the walls. Automatic transmission is rare and
       expensive in Italy: if you need one, book it now, not later.
     · ZTL everywhere: park OUTSIDE the walls at Siena, San Gimignano, Montepulciano,
       Montalcino, Buonconvento, Radda. Camera fines arrive by post months later.
 - Lodging: all three countryside stays are BOOKED. Still open — the Florence arrival night
   (3–4 Oct) only.
 - The Argentario house is SELF-CATERING and up on the hill at Pozzarello, ~2.5 km above Porto
   Santo Stefano harbour. Shop on the way in on Day 2; the harbour is a 5-min drive for dinner.
 - Montelodoli Capanna: stone house for two, 16 m infinity pool 40 m from the door (season runs
   to October — confirm it's still filled and worth swimming in for 8–14 Oct), A/C, heating
   billed on use. Nearest shop is the COOP in Gaiole, 11 km.
 - Locanda in Tuscany sits at Gallina on the Via Cassia, which puts you 17 min from Bagno
   Vignoni, 25 from Bagni San Filippo, 30 from Pienza, 40 from Montepulciano and 40 from
   Montalcino — the whole Val d'Orcia inside 45 minutes. Book the Taverna di Mozart for at
   least one night, and ask about the Brunello/Vino Nobile tastings they arrange.
 - The LAST NIGHT is a transit night, not a hotel night: after the Florence shopping day take
   an evening Frecciarossa to Milano Centrale (~2 h; the ~20:30–21:30 departures are the sweet
   spot), then a late Malpensa Express or night bus to MXP (airport buses run through the
   night). Terminal 1/2 stay open — aim to be at the airport by ~05:00 for the 07:00 flight.
 - Trains: two train moments — Day 1 (Milan → Florence on arrival) and Day 15 evening
   (Florence → Milano Centrale → Malpensa). Fares open ~90–120 days out; grab cheap
   "Super Economy" fares early (€15–20 vs €70–80 last-minute).
 - Seasonal: the Giardino dei Tarocchi closes for the season 15 Oct — with the beach first
   (Day 3) you're comfortably inside the window. The Argentario fishing harbours stay open
   year-round. Montalcino's weekly market is FRIDAY, which is why it's Day 14.
 - Baggage: the dawn budget flight is strict — ship a case of wine home (arrange it Day 14 in
   Montalcino, or Day 8 at Coltibuono) or pre-book a checked bag.
